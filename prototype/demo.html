What the full platform does

Crop advice by SMSFor each stage of rice and cassava, timed to the month each farmer plants; held back when heavy rain is forecast.
Pest Doctor and hotspotsFarmers report what they see; 3 reports of one pest in a county make a hotspot, and officers get an SMS.
Ask an ExpertQuestions by SMS, *347# or the app, with a photo or a call-back request, routed to the right officer; escalated after 24 hours.
Consent firstLead farmers and officers register farmers, who join only by replying YES, and can stop any time with STOP.
Weather and market prices5-day forecast and farm advice per county; prices reported by trusted market reporters.
Built for a real pilotPersonal staff logins with 2-step login, officers limited to their counties, audit log, daily checked backups.

This demo uses sample data and sends no SMS. The full platform runs on a server with a real SMS/USSD provider. RETRAP AgTech Innovation Hackathon 2026, Group 10 · Source code on GitHub
