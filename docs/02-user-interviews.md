# 2 · Talk to real users — interview guide & field notes

Goal: speak to **at least two** farmers, extension officers or traders before Thursday 19 November, 6:00 pm.

## Before the interview
- Introduce yourself and the hackathon. Ask permission to take notes (and photos, if they agree).
- Keep it to about 15 minutes. Listen more than you talk. Don't pitch the app first.

## Questions for farmers
1. What did you grow last season, and how did it go?
2. Where do you get farming advice today? Who do you trust most?
3. What was your biggest loss last year — pests, rain, price, something else?
4. Tell me about the last time a pest or disease attacked your crop. What did you do?
5. How do you know the price before you sell?
6. What phone do you use? Do you use SMS, USSD (`*…#`), WhatsApp, radio?
7. Which language would you like to hear advice in?
8. Would you pay for advice? How much, and how?

## Questions for extension officers
1. How many farmers / towns do you cover? How do you travel?
2. What questions do farmers ask most?
3. How do you hear about pest outbreaks today?
4. What would help you reach more farmers?

## Questions for traders / agro-dealers
1. How do farmers find your shop or you find farmers?
2. How are prices set at your market?
3. Would you pay to list your shop or post prices?
