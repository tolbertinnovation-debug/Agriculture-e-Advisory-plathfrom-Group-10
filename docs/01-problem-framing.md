# 1 · Frame the problem — AgriConnect Liberia

**RETRAP AgTech Innovation Hackathon · Group 10 · Pre-work due Thursday 19 November 2026, 6:00 pm**

## Our user
Smallholder farmers in rural Liberia growing rice, cassava, cocoa, oil palm and vegetables. Most farm small plots with family labour, many use a basic (non-smart) phone, and many are more comfortable speaking Liberian English or a local language (Kpelle, Bassa, Kissi, Gio, Mano…) than reading.

**Persona — Kollie, Salala District, Bong County.** Grows upland rice, cassava and pepper. Owns a basic phone. Gets advice from neighbours, the radio and occasionally an extension officer who visits rarely.

## Their pain point
Farmers make the big decisions of the season — what to plant, when to plant, when to spray, how to treat a sick crop, where and when to sell — **without timely, local, trusted information**.

- Extension officers are few and cover very large areas, so most farmers rarely see one.
- Pests and diseases (fall armyworm, cassava mosaic, cocoa black pod, rice blast) spread before anyone notices.
- Heavy rain washes away fertilizer and sprays applied at the wrong time.
- Farmers don't know market prices, so middlemen set the price.

## Our solution in one sentence
**AgriConnect Liberia** is one advisory engine that delivers local, timely farming advice by smartphone app (offline), USSD (`*347#` placeholder), SMS and voice in local languages, with a dashboard that lets extension officers answer questions and send alerts to thousands of farmers at once.
